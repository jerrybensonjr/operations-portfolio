## Core Capabilities

* **5-Day SOP Sprints:** Converting unstructured memos, Loom recordings, and fragmented notes into execution-ready checklists.
* **Process Standardization:** Flat documentation architectures with clear ownership and zero nested clutter.
* **Workflow Logic:** Designing auditable, async-first operational handoffs for growing businesses.

## Operating Principles

1. **Active Over Passive:** Concrete verbs and explicit operational thresholds over corporate jargon.
2. **Flat Over Nested:** Information architecture designed to be located and executed in under 30 seconds.
3. **Auditability:** Every workflow includes defined inputs, clear trigger events, and verifiable exit criteria.
